How should your tabs be grouped?

Pick your categories

Suggested from your bookmark folders and open tabs. Each one you keep becomes a folder.

Put your tabs down the side

TabSage works best with Chrome's tabs down the side of the window, where every group and the tabs in it stay in view, and the Recent side panel (Alt+Shift+R) beside the page.
  • Set Tab position to show tabs on the side.
  • Set Side panel position to whichever side you want the Recent panel on.
Chrome only lets you change these yourself, so TabSage opens the page for you. You can skip this step.
